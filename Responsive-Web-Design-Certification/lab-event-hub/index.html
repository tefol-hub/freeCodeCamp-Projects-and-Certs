Trip to the Pyramids of Giza

The Pyramids of Giza are a complex of ancient Egyptian monuments on the Giza Plateau, built around (2600-2500) BC during the Fourth Dynasty to serve as tombs for pharaohs. The three main pyramids are the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the Pyramid of Khafre, and the Pyramid of Menkaure. The complex also includes the Great Sphinx, smaller satellite pyramids, and various temples and tombs.*

Date:25 August 2025

Trip to The Pantheon

The Pantheon is a former Roman temple in Rome, Italy, now a Catholic church, famous for its architectural marvels like the largest unreinforced concrete dome in the world and its central oculus. Originally commissioned by Marcus Agrippa and rebuilt by Emperor Hadrian around 126-128 AD, it was dedicated to all the Roman gods and has become one of the best-preserved ancient Roman buildings, influencing countless architectural designs throughout history.*
